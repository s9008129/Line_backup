The original task, issue, c6 invitation, review receipts, source snapshots, and writer receipts must remain immutable historical evidence. A new lineage must explicitly cite the old task and issue as an unresolved predecessor and must not imply the issue was resolved. If the unchanged controller cannot represent that predecessor relationship without bypassing the issue guard, stop and return for a planner/controller decision; do not allocate under a renamed task as a workaround.
